Service 1

Automation

Connect your apps and remove repetitive workflows. Information arrives, gets checked against your rules, lands in the right place, and the right person is told what needs attention.

Supplier invoicesOrder emailsCSV / Excel importsPrice listsAlerts & reminders

Problems it solves

  • The same data typed twice: from a PDF or email into Excel, then into the accounting software
  • Errors found weeks later, such as wrong VAT, duplicate invoices or totals that don't add up
  • Staff time lost on copy-paste instead of supplier and customer follow-up
  • Nobody is alerted when something goes wrong, so problems pile up silently

How it works

Receive

Files dropped in a folder, email attachments, or exports from another system.

Extract

Read the fields. For scanned documents, optional OCR or a local AI model suggests the values.

Validate

Your business rules check every record: dates, VAT, totals, currencies, mandatory fields, duplicates.

Save

Valid records go to the database or your application. Invalid ones are quarantined with a reason.

Notify

A summary by email or Telegram: how many were processed, and exactly what needs a person.

Worked example: supplier invoice processing

The situation
Medina Food Distribution (fictional, 45 employees) receives about 300 supplier invoices a month from 25 suppliers. An accounting assistant types each one into a spreadsheet, checks the VAT with a calculator, then enters it again into the accounting software.

Before

  • ~4 minutes per invoice → about 20 hours a month
  • Errors noticed at month-end closing
  • Duplicate invoices sometimes paid twice
  • No record of what was checked

After

  • Suppliers' files are processed as soon as they arrive
  • Only rejected rows need a person: about 2 hours a month
  • Duplicates blocked automatically
  • Every run is logged with counts and reasons

Visualise

Report

A 2-page monthly PDF that answers your key questions.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do we have to replace our ERP or accounting software?

No. We work around what you already have: exports, emails, shared folders. When your software offers an import or an API, we can feed data into it directly.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

AI never has the final word. It only suggests values (for example, from a scanned invoice), and the same validation rules check them. Anything uncertain is sent to a person. The system also works with AI turned off.

Is our data safe?

Demos use synthetic data. For pilots, your data stays in an environment we agree on together, access is limited by role, every change is logged, and the data handling is written into the agreement.

Can we buy only one service?

Yes. That is how most clients start. Each service is designed to work on its own.

What happens after the pilot?

You keep what was delivered. You can then extend it to more processes, add another service, or take a monthly support plan, or stop there.

Who owns the code and the workflows?

This is agreed in the proposal. Typically, the client receives full rights to what was built specifically for them, while reusable building blocks remain ours, licensed to them.

Which task takes your team too much time?

Tell us about one process. If there's a fit, you'll see a working demo on your own sample data.
